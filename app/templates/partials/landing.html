Explainable hematology pattern analysis.

01

Research context

Academic pattern exploration

HemaLens uses a research XGBoost model to explore patterns from six hematology parameters. The interface is intended for academic demonstration and model-transparency work.

02

Clinical boundary

Not for clinical diagnosis

HemaLens must not be used for diagnosis, clinical screening, treatment decisions, or other medical decision-making. It does not replace standard laboratory testing or evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.

Model scores are internal research outputs, not clinical probabilities.
